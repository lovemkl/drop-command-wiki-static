Mech & unit loadout planner

Loadout planner using only what the Steam page publishes: you can pilot any unit under your command (Mechs, Tanks, Infantry, Aircraft, with VTOL and Battle Armour named for customization), fight alone, lead a squad or command an army, level up pilots, and chase strategic resources, pivotal systems, manufacturing hubs and political objectives. Weapon, part and stat names are not listed on Steam, so refit slots use broad focus labels plus Unknown. Shareable URL state. Confirm exact options in the Drop Command client — this planner does not invent stats or IDs.

Related: Getting started · Unit types · FAQ

How to use

  1. Optional: tap a preset chip (planner aid — not an official PillBugGames build).
  2. Or choose each slot below. Use Unknown when unsure.
  3. Add short notes if needed, then Copy link to share (query params encode every slot, checklist, and notes).
  4. Opening a shared URL auto-applies selections; use Apply from URL to re-read the address bar, or Clear to start over.

Mech & unit loadout planner

Pick the unit you pilot (Mech, Tank, Infantry, Aircraft/VTOL or Battle Armour), set a refit focus, choose your squad support, pilot approach, campaign objective and diplomacy stance. Pick Unknown when the official list is unclear. No fabricated stats. Presets are planner aids only.

Presets (planner aids — not official builds)

  • Rookie rush: Planner aid: Steam’s “rush your opponent with disposable rookies” idea — soft only.
  • Honest Unknown: All Unknown — fill after your first mission.

Accuracy policy

  • Unit types (Mechs, Tanks, Infantry, Aircraft; VTOL and Battle Armour for customization), command scale, pilot levelling, objectives, rivals and diplomacy all come from the Steam description.
  • Steam does not list weapons, parts, chassis names or stats. “Firepower / Armour / Speed” are our planning labels, not in-game menus.
  • Squad sizes, costs and pilot trait names are not published on Steam, so we do not show them.
  • Presets are soft suggestions only — not official builds from PillBugGames.
