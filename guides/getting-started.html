Getting started

Steam: ‘90s mech combat at a grand scale. Pilot Mechs, Tanks, Infantry and Aircraft in combined arms combat, and rise to command whole battalions in a sandbox campaign.

  • Get it on Steam (app 1944880). A free demo is on the store page.
  • Drop into combat alone, leading your squad, or commanding an army (Steam).
  • Pilot any unit under your command (Steam).
  • Mission results carry over into the campaign (Steam).
  • Plan units in the loadout planner; pick Unknown if you are not sure.

The core loop

Steam: fight battles from small skirmishes to epic fights, conquer rivals and build a stellar empire. The battlefield changes as you fight, with fully destructible debris and unit wreckage.

Options

Steam lists adjustable difficulty, playable without timed input, camera comfort, colour alternatives and custom volume controls.

Release status

Released on Steam on Sep 28, 2026 (checked Sep 29, 2026, 09:03 CST). US store price: $22.99 USD, with an introductory offer of $19.54 (−15%) until October 11. Prices differ by region — check the Steam store page.

Steam lists it as a full release (not Early Access).

What we will not make up

No weapon stats, part lists, unit costs or faction names that Steam does not publish.
